A beautiful dua for Ali

☾
اللَّهُمَّ احْفَظْهُ وَبَارِكْ فِيهِ

May Allah protect him
and bless his life.

Ya Allah, bless little Ali with good health, a long and joyful life, a kind heart, beautiful character and beneficial knowledge. Keep him safe from every harm, guide him towards goodness, and fill his life with Your mercy and barakah.

Make him the coolness of his parents' eyes, a comfort to their hearts, and a source of goodness for everyone around him.

Ameen, ya Rabb-ul-Alameen
